Project Objective

The Tuition Management System is a centralized platform designed to automate and manage tuition/coaching institute operations. The system handles student admissions, slab-wise fee management, attendance tracking, examinations, answer sheet scanning, result generation, and reporting.

Core Modules

Admission Management

  • Student Registration
  • Document Upload
  • Batch Allocation
  • Admission Approval

Slab Wise Fee Management

  • Fee Slab Configuration
  • Discount Management
  • Installments
  • Scholarship Support

Payment Collection

  • Cash / UPI / Bank Transfer
  • Receipt Generation
  • Outstanding Tracking
  • Payment History

Attendance

  • Daily Attendance
  • Batch Attendance
  • Attendance Reports

Examination

  • Exam Creation
  • Marks Entry
  • Subject Management

Answer Sheet Scanner

  • PDF Upload
  • Image Upload
  • OCR Processing
  • Student Mapping

Result Generation

  • Percentage Calculation
  • Grade Calculation
  • Report Cards
  • Performance Analysis

Reports & Dashboard

  • Admissions
  • Fee Collection
  • Attendance Reports
  • Result Analytics

Admission Process Flow

``` Student Inquiry ↓ Registration ↓ Document Verification ↓ Admission Approval ↓ Batch Allocation ↓ Fee Slab Assignment ↓ Payment Collection
```
